🌹 Rose Day 🌹

Like a rose that blooms in the garden of my heart,
you bring color to my every day.

Your beauty isn't just what I see,
it's the grace in your laughter,
the warmth in your voice,
and the kindness in your soul.

Just as a rose needs the sun to bloom,
I need you to feel complete.

Every petal represents a reason I admire you,
and there are countless roses in my heart,
because my reasons are infinite.

You are my eternal spring, Radha. 🌹
