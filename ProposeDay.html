💖 Propose Day 💖

Today, I want to tell you what my heart has known all along.

From the moment you walked into my life,
something shifted.
The world became brighter,
my days became meaningful,
and my heart found its home.

I don't just love you, Radha.
I choose you every single day.

I choose your laughter when I need joy,
your strength when I feel weak,
your presence when I feel lost.

Will you be mine?
Not just for today, not just for this week,
but for all the tomorrows we'll create together?

My heart is yours, completely and forever. 💕
