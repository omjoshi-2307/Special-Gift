💋 Kiss Day 💋

A kiss is more than just a touch of lips,
it's a promise, a connection, a moment frozen in time.

When I think of kissing you,
I think of all the unspoken words between us,
all the emotions that overflow,
all the love that can't be contained.

I want to kiss away your doubts,
to seal every promise with tenderness,
to show you with every touch
how deeply you're cherished.

Your lips hold the kind of magic
that makes time stand still,
that makes my heart race,
that makes everything else fade away.

I long for those stolen moments,
those sweet embraces,
those kisses that say everything words cannot. 💋💕
